More about me...

Hi, I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Birmingham, originally based in Dubai.

Cars and the machines that move people have been my way into engineering since before I knew the word "torque" — and that same fascination is still what drives me to pursue my dream of designing and building automotive systems - among other projects - that make a real difference for the world, and the people who use them.

To me, one of the most important aspects of engineering is considering who's on the other end of the design. A faster gearbox is interesting. A gearbox that lets a specific person drive again and commute more safely - that's the project I actually want to design and develop.
